##  About the Game
Cave Escape is a 2D platformer game developed in Unity using C#. Explore a dangerous cave filled with monsters, obstacles, and hidden treasures. Fight your way through the cave, collect useful items, and find the keys needed to escape.

## Controls
- **A / Left Arrow:** Move Left
- **D / Right Arrow:** Move Right
- **Space:** Jump
- **Left Mouse Button:** Attack

##  Gameplay
Your objective is to explore the cave, defeat monsters, collect keys, and find a way to escape.

Along the way, you can:
- Fight enemies using your attack abilities.
- Explore the cave and overcome obstacles.
- Collect coins to increase your score.
- Find keys required to unlock your escape.
- Collect power-ups to improve your character's abilities.

##  Collectibles & Power-Ups

### Collectibles
- **Coins:** Increase your score when collected.

### Power-Ups
- **Health:** Restores a portion of your health.
- **Damage Boost:** Temporarily increases your attack damage.
- **Jump Boost:** Increases your jumping ability.
- **Invincibility:** Makes the player temporarily immune to damage.

## Enemies
The cave is inhabited by dangerous monsters that will try to stop you from escaping. Defeat them using your attacks and carefully manage your health to survive.

##  Objective
Explore the cave, defeat its monsters, collect coins and power-ups, and gather the necessary keys to unlock your escape.

**Survive the dangers, find the keys, and escape the cave!**
